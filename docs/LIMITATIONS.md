# Scope of claims

V00 retains E/S/T scoring and auxiliary losses. All five seeds show positive full-validation fixed-weight deletion contributions for each field. Mean Long S contribution remains -0.012508 (only2/5 positive), so universal harmlessness on long segments is not established. Late training degrades: final-epoch mean Full MR is53.876635, versus best57.260716. Do not report best as final-epoch stability.

The five seeds were used in development comparisons. Small advantages are not statistical significance or independent confirmation. V00 was locked before testing55 historical checkpoints. Its small validation advantage did not transfer to test; do not retrospectively choose another recipe from that test comparison.

Existing L02 factorial and cross-dataset runs cannot be relabelled as V00. The package does not establish complete experimental evidence, unseen-seed confirmation, full retraining ablations, or superiority under all feature/pretraining conditions. Cleanup equivalence tests cover specific trajectories and checkpoints, not all possible inputs or GPU determinism.
